# Assisted mode: the replies allowed to go without a tap

In assisted mode a draft sends on its own only if it contains no digit, no currency symbol, no date word, is under the word limit, the thread has no attachment, AND it says essentially the same thing as one of the templates below. Everything else waits for you.

Keep this list short. Every template here is something you would be comfortable seeing sent in your name at 3am with no one reading it first.
